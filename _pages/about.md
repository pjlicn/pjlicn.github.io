I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at [the Pennsylvania State University](https://www.psu.edu/), University Park, working with the [MHPI](https://sites.google.com/view/mhpi/team) team. I earned my Ph.D. in Hydrological Sciences.

I combine hydrological process understanding, multi-source observations, and differentiable and data-driven models to study water systems across scales.

## Research areas

- Hybrid and differentiable hydrological modeling.
- Data assimilation, bias identification, and state estimation.
- Cross-scale surface-water, soil-moisture, and groundwater analysis.

## Current research directions

I am developing multi-scale hydrological simulations that integrate complementary observations and represent human influences on water systems. Current work includes using SWOT data to improve hydrological modeling and incorporating reservoir operations into models.
